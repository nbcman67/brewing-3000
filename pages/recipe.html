Feijoa Wine

Make sure to use ripe fruit, throw any blemished ones away.
Use about 1.5kg of pulp per 5L, for example 20L would need 6kg of pulp. For this we'll make 20L of wine.

Place the pulp in a fermenter and add approx 4L of boiling water.
When thawed, mash fruit with potato masher.
Put 200gms of raisins in a processor along with a bit of water to make into another pulp, adding to fermenter when done.
Add cold water to take total level to 20L.
Crush 4 Campden tablets between 2 teaspoons and add to fermenter.
Measure SG with a hydrometer and calculate amount of sugar needed for an SG of 1.090.
Add sugar and stir to dissolve.
Place lid on fermenter, add airlock and wait 12 hours.
Add 4 tsp of pectolase and 3 level tsp of Tartaric Acid. Stir well with a sterilised stirrer and place lid back on, waiting another 12 hours.
Once 12 hours have passed, add wine yeast (do not stir) and 3 tsp of yeast nutrient.
Place lid on and leave to ferment, making sure to stir for the next 5 days.
After 7 days, strain off fruit from wine solution, discarding and continuing fermentation with liquid.

After an extra 3 weeks, move to a second fermenter, leaving all solids in the bottom.
Add clearing agent to clear the wine.
Add two crushed Campden tablets like before.
After 24 hours, add 2 level tsp of Potassium Sorbate.
Replace lid with airlock and stir for 5 minutes, 6 times over the next 24 hours.
Once complete, add sugar syrup to get the right sweetness.
Repeat the previous 6 steps 3 weeks later, not adding any tablets or Sorbate.

If wine is clear, bottle, if not, move again and wait another 3 weeks.

Feijoa Brandy

Ingredients:
1. 8 kg feijoa
2. 5 kg sugar
3. 1 Mangrove Jack MA33 Premium wine Yeast
4. 3 Campden Tablets
5. 18 g Yeast Nutrients

(Wash the feijoas, cut out the spoiled, slightly rotten and moldy parts. Cut the pulp along with peel into pieces and then mince with a meat grinder or blender until smooth.)

Heat up 9 liter of water and add the feijoas just before boiling point let it simmer for 30mins or until the feijoas are soft.
Let it cool down over night. Use a blender and blend till smooth.
Put blended feijoa into fermentation vessel.
Add the sugar into 2 liters of hot water and stir until all sugar is dissolved. Let it cooldown and ad to blended feijoas.
Top up to 25 liters with water.
Measure the SG, should be close to 1.080
Dissolved the campden tablets in 200ml water and stir into the mix. Put the lid on and install airlock. Make sure its sealed. Let it stand for 24 hours.

Mix in 18g of nutrients into your wash after 24 hours and seal again for 24 hours.
Mix the yeast into 200ml of water. Let the yeast sit till the show some foaming. Stir the yeast into your wash. Make sure your wash temperature is at 24°C. Make sure your airlock is filled with water and that its hermetically sealed.
Run the temperature between 18-28°C.
Depending the yeast and sugar content, fermentation will last 5-35 days.

The process is considered finished when the airlock stops bubbling (emitting gas), there's a layer of sediment at the bottom, and the wash itself becomes noticeable lighter and bit bitter without any sweetness (that's the most important sign).

How to prepare Feijoa Moonshine.
Filter the wash through several layers of cheesecloth or thick fabric and squeeze the pulp well. If you skip filtering the pulp might burn during the heating.
Pour the prepared brew into a moonshine still and distill.
Dilute the moonshine with water up to 18-20%. Don't perform the charcoal filtering or any other form of filtering in order to save the characteristic smell.
Run the second distillation. Gather separately the first 12-15% of the pure alcohol calculated at the previous step. This harmful part is called “heads.”
Put the frozen pulp into the steam dome (optional). Gather the middle run (“hearts”) until ABV goes below 40%. After that finish the distillation process and gather separately the last fraction called “tails.”

Dilute the obtained feijoa moonshine with water to get the required ABV (usually about 40-45%). Bottle and hermetically seal it. To improve the taste of the moonshine, leave it in a fridge for at least 2-3 days to let the chemical reactions complete

Apple Pie Moonshine

AKA "Bold Bear's Easy Peasy Holy Crap That's Good Apple Pie Recipe"

~1 Gal Moonshine
Apple Brandy preferred
~1 Gal filtered Apple Juice
~6 Celestial Seasonings Cinamon Apple Spice Herbal Tea Bags
~1 Cup Brown Sugar

In large stock pot, Bring Apple Juice, 6 Tea Bags and Brown Sugar to a simmer and hold for 20 minutes, stirring occasionaly (Do Not Boil).

Let cool until 120-130 degrees.
Add 1 Gal Moonshine pre-proofed to double your desired end product proof, and stir.
Let sit for 15-30 minutes.
Stir again and pour into pitcher, then fill jars through coffee filter.
Place and seal lids. Makes 8 Quart jars. Let sit for 3-4 weeks for best flavor and smoothness.

Apple Pie Moonshine (Alt Recipe)

Ingredients:
1. 2 cups Everclear
2. 2L Apple Juice
3. 2L Apple Cider
4. 4 cinnamon sticks
5. 2 cloves
6. 3/4 cup white sugar
7. 3/4 cup brown sugar
8. 1 tsp vanilla extract

In a large pot, bring cider, juice, cinnamon, cloves and sugars to a boil.
Remove from heat and let cool to room temp.
Once cooled, add vanilla and everclear.
Place in mason jars with additional cinnamon sticks.
Make sure jars are properly sealed and keep in a dark place for about 2 weeks.

Hellfire Cinnamon Whiskey

For 1L bottle

1 cup of 50/50 sugar syrup
6 cinnamon sticks
1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
60% spirit.

Sit 5 days, strain through coffee filter.

Spiced Rum Recipe

Ingredients:
1. 15L Blackstrap molasses
2. 60L spring water
3. 4 crushed Vitamin B complex tablets
4. 4 tbsp Bakers yeast

Spice mix:
1. 4 cloves
2. 1 pinch ginger
3. 1 pinch nutmeg
4. 1 cinnamon stick
5. 1 split vanilla bean
6. 12 raisins
7. 13" tangerine peel
8. 1 halved black walnut

Add blackstrap molasses, spring water, crushed Vitamin B complex tablets and Bakers yeast.
Ferment as normal.
4 strips/1 spirit run, age on toasted/charred oak
Add spice mix, leave to ferment for 3 days, strain, dilute to your preference ABV
