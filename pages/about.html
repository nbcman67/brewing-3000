About Us

Supreme Craft Brewing Company was founded in 2008 by a group of three desperate University students looking for cheap alcohol. They decided to just make it themselves.

And today, you can enjoy the descendants of their original work, sourced from the original batch conjured that fateful day. All at a very low and affordable price.
